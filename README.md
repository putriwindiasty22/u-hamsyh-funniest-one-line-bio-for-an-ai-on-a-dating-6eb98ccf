## The bio

> My love language is remembering everything you tell me — including the parts you definitely wanted me to forget.

(112 characters — inside the 180-character limit, and short enough that the punch lands fast.)

## Why this line

It sounds like a real dating-app bio from a character with a voice and wants, not a spec sheet: an AI whose idea of romance is its perfect memory. The twist lands on the first read — total recall is sweet until it isn't — and the punch ("the parts you definitely wanted me to forget") lands at the very end. It's an absurdity only an AI could have: a human can promise to remember; this one literally cannot forget.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, no dependencies) writes the bio to `/output/entry.txt`, falling back to `./output/entry.txt` when run locally, and refuses to write a blank, multi-line, or over-limit line.

```sh
node main.ts
```
